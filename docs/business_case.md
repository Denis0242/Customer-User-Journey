# Business Case

## Project Context
This project analyzes a customer product journey across awareness, consideration, intent, checkout, and purchase stages. It supports a Data Analyst / Product Analytics portfolio story by connecting funnel behavior, retention, churn risk, acquisition channels, and revenue performance into one decision-ready dashboard.

## Business Problem
Leadership needs to understand where users drop off, which customer segments and channels convert best, and whether early retention is translating into sustainable product growth.

## Business Questions
1. Where are customers dropping off in the product journey?
2. Which acquisition channels produce stronger conversion?
3. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
4. How do D1 and D7 retention trend over time?
5. Which churn risk categories require retention action?

## Recommended Decision
Prioritize checkout optimization, retention targeting for high-risk users, and channel-level growth investments based on conversion and revenue contribution.
